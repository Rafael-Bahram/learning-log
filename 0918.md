## E03 Linear Transformation and Matrices ｜2026-09-18

1. 【In One Word】A 2x2 Matrix represents a linear transformation : its two columns are the destination of the basis vectors after transformation.
2. 【Interger Intuition】Transformation Matrix:R=[a,c][b,d],Origin point P=[x,y] ,R*P=[ax+by,cx+dy]
3. 【Geometrical Intuition】Matrix represents the position of the basis vectors after transformation ,and the whole grid gets "strectched and reshaped", while the origin stays fixed ,and straight lines remain straight.

## E04 Matrix Multiplication as Composition｜2026-09-18

1. 【In One Word】Matrix multiplication equals to composition transformation ,and the operations apply from right to left ,which is ABv represents vector v first apply transformation B first and then apply transformation A.
2. 【Interger Intuition】Matrix: M1=[a,b][c,d] , M2=[e,f][g,h] , M1*M2=[ae+cf,ce+df][ag+ch,bg+dh] ,so M1*M2≠M2*M1 ,and A(BC)=(AB)C.
3. 【Geometrical Intuition】Matrix multiplication is a composition of linear transformations , which means the basis vectors apply the transformation step by step from right to left.
